Overview

What this service covers

We set up and automate the systems behind outbound: the data, the sequences, the routing and the reporting. Your reps spend their time in conversations, and your leaders see what’s working.

What’s included

01

Outbound sequencing setup

Build multi-step outreach sequences across email and other channels, ready to run and measure.

02

Lead enrichment and scoring

Add the data reps need and score leads by fit and intent so effort goes to the right accounts.

03

CRM workflow automation

Automate stages, tasks and updates so the CRM reflects reality without manual upkeep.

04

Lead routing

Assign leads by territory, size or round-robin rules, with response-time tracking.
