Workflow Automation for Obvi: 35 Ops Hours Saved Per Week and 30% Fewer Support Tickets

Case study summary

Executive summary

We built event-driven automation that detects order exceptions across Shopify, ERP and 3PL, triggers refunds, reships or supplier alerts automatically, syncs inventory in real time, and notifies the customer — saving 35 ops hours per week and cutting order-status support tickets by 30%.

The challenge

Order exceptions, returns, inventory mismatches and carrier delays at Obvi were handled entirely by hand — spreadsheets, email chains and manual checks across Shopify, their ERP and 3PL portal. Every exception took an ops team member off more important work.

Customers found out about problems when they asked. Inventory often wasn't synced in time to prevent oversells. The team was reactive by design — there was no system to be proactive.

What we built

Event-driven automation that listens to Shopify, ERP and 3PL simultaneously. When an exception fires — return, stockout, carrier delay, address problem — the right action runs automatically: trigger a refund, reship from closest warehouse, alert the supplier, or notify the customer with a new ETA.

Inventory syncs in real time across systems to prevent oversells. Routine exceptions resolve without touching ops. The team sees a clean queue of genuine edge cases instead of a firehose.

The result

  • 35 ops hours saved per week on exception handling.
  • Order-status support tickets down 30%.
  • Fewer oversells through real-time inventory sync.
  • Ops team focused on edge cases, not routine exceptions.
