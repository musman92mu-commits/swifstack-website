Overview

What we do in insurance

Insurance runs on documents: submissions, ACORD forms, loss runs, claims reports. Most of it arrives unstructured and is re-keyed by hand, which slows every decision and invites errors. We build systems that read, check and file those documents, and keep a person in the loop wherever judgment or regulation requires it.
