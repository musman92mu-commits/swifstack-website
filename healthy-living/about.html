Our Story

About Healthy Living International

22+ years of natural health. One person. Real support.

Founded in 2002

Built on a principle, not a trend.

Healthy Living International was founded in Orem, Utah in 2002 by Kent Hinkson, inspired by the work and principles of Dr. William R. Kellas. What started as a mission to share Dr. Kellas's formulations has grown into a trusted community of health-focused customers across 7 countries.

"The key to health is eliminating toxicities and deficiencies."
— Dr. William R. Kellas, Founding Principle

This simple but profound principle guides every product we carry, every recommendation we make, and every conversation Kent has with customers. Not symptoms. Root causes.

Our customers stay with us. Many have been purchasing for 5, 10, even 19 years — not because of lock-in or subscriptions, but because they see results and because Kent actually answers the phone.

Our Journey

22+ years building trust

2002
Founded in Orem, Utah
Kent launches Healthy Living International to bring Dr. Kellas's formulations to people nationwide. The original product lineup focuses on foundational nutrition.
2005–2010
Expanding the catalog
The product range grows to 30+ SKUs across multiple health categories. Word of mouth drives loyalty — customers refer friends and family based on personal results.
2012
International shipping begins
Demand from Australia, Canada, and Singapore prompts international shipping. Kent handles customer questions from every timezone personally.
2015–2020
55+ products, 16 categories
The catalog expands to cover a comprehensive range of health needs. Products like PXP Forte and Miror EPF are added for advanced users seeking deeper support.
2026
Still going strong
Serving customers in 7+ countries. Many customers have been purchasing for over a decade. Kent still personally answers every call and email.
